Updated journey flow (what students experience now)

Search no longer jumps straight into the animated career map. Profile collection comes first; the animation and payment follow after.

1. Homepage search / Explore → 2. Deep profile (short form + mobile OTP) → 3. Animated career journey → 4. Begin your journey → Payment → 5. Fresh class-based journey path

Earlier flow: Homepage → Journey map. That redirect has been replaced by Homepage → Profile collection.

Day 1

Monday, 3 August 2026

Homepage

  • New Career professionals banner — A multi-professional image strip was added above the black strip on the homepage (professionals across careers).

Site-wide logo

  • New Merrakii logo everywhere — The same logo from the homepage now appears on all non-animation pages, and also at the top of the animation page.

Plans & payment

  • Updated Three fixed journey plans — The plans screen now shows only Starter (₹99), Growth (₹499), and Premium (₹999), each with a Continue button. The old “Choose a subscription / per month” panel was removed.
  • Removed Fewer fields on checkout — Full name, mobile, and later email were removed from the journey payment page so checkout feels lighter.
  • New Payment method tiles with real icons — Checkout shows branded tiles for UPI, cards, wallets, net banking, and related methods, styled to match the Merrakii+ theme.
  • New UPI Generate QR flow — Selecting UPI shows a Generate QR experience. A 30-second timer runs above the QR. At 18 seconds a success popup appears: “Congratulations, payment successful!” with a View Journey button. When the timer ends, the QR returns to the generate state. Extra UPI app shortcut buttons (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm, etc.) were removed from this panel.
  • New Card OTP screen — After card details and Start Free Trial, the student is taken to a card OTP confirmation page. Card expiry input auto-formats as MM/YY.
  • New Net banking bank screen — Choosing a bank and starting the trial opens a dedicated net-banking confirmation-style page.
  • Removed “Verify it’s you” email OTP page deleted — That intermediate email-OTP screen no longer appears in the flow.
Day 2

Tuesday, 4 August 2026

Redirection & entry path

  • Updated Homepage search → profile first — When a student searches from the homepage, they are redirected to the deep profiling (profile collection) page first, then to the animated journey. Previously, search went straight to the journey / map.
  • Updated Begin your journey → payment — On the animation page, Begin your journey sends the student to the payment gateway (plans / checkout), instead of opening the old slide-in free-trial panel.

Deep profile page (profile collection)

This is the “confused student” screen on the left and the short form on the right.

  • New Confused student artwork — A student illustration was added on the left half of the profile page, with thought-bubble questions rising from the student’s mind: “How can I achieve my career goal?”, “What jobs will be in high demand…?”, “What is right for me?”, “Which entrance exam…?”.
  • New Service tiles under the student — Below the student: “Not sure how to get your dream job or build your dream startup?” plus four image-based tiles — Career Counselling, Test Prep, 21st Century Skills, Industry & Mentor Matching.
  • Updated Fields reduced — The form keeps only: Full name, Phone number, Email address, and Class. Board, school, city, parent details, and other long fields were removed.
  • New Get OTP → Proceed — Students tap Get OTP, receive a mobile OTP, enter the code, then the button becomes Proceed. Correct OTP takes them to the animation page.
  • New Student logo above the form — A student logo sits above the separator line over the student-details section.
  • Updated Phone field with country code — Phone input shows a flag + dial code pattern (example number 98345 28374).

Animated career journey (Civil Engineer & related)

  • New Wooden trunks on every milestone — A wooden trunk marker sits on each milestone platform (Class 9 through post-graduation).
  • New “Begin your journey” button — Fixed near the bottom centre of the animation page. This is the exit into payment.
  • Removed Slide-in free-trial side panel — Clicking a milestone no longer opens the right-hand panel with Free Trial.
  • New Hover thought-bubble popups — Hovering a milestone shows a cloud-shaped thought bubble with milestone-related info.
  • Updated Character movement — The student character moves with arrow keys between milestones and shows the thought bubble when arriving at a milestone.
Day 3

Wednesday, 5 August 2026

Deep profile

  • New Resend OTP with 30-second timer — After Get OTP, Resend stays inactive while a 30s timer runs; then Resend activates. Clicking Resend restarts the cooldown.
  • New Country code dropdown — The flag / +91 control is a full country dropdown (India listed first), not a static badge.

Animation page — controls

  • New On-screen D / reverse-D arrow buttons — Previous / next controls sit together under “Begin your journey”, matching keyboard arrows.

After payment — View Journey

  • Updated View Journey opens a fresh class-based path — After payment success, students see a fresh journey starting from the class they entered on the profile page, for the career they searched.
  • New U-shaped class strips with trunks & services — Each class is a strip with a trunk at alternating ends (right, left, right…). Strips connect in a continuous path (Class 10 → 11 → 12 → graduation). Each strip shows the services that unlock that milestone.

At-a-glance highlights

  1. Homepage: multi-professional banner added.
  2. Redirect: Homepage → Journey changed to Homepage → Deep profile → Animation → Payment → Class journey.
  3. Profile page: confused-student art + thought bubbles; form cut down to name, phone, email, class; mobile OTP gate.
  4. Animation: trunks on milestones, Begin your journey, thought-bubble popups, arrow navigation; free-trial side panel gone.
  5. Payment: three fixed plans, branded method tiles, UPI QR timer + success popup, card OTP and net-banking screens.
  6. Post-pay journey: fresh U-path of class strips with services, starting from the student’s profile class and searched career.
